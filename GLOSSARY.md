# Happy 3rd Year, My Love

A scroll-driven website given to the creator's girlfriend on their 3rd anniversary. As she scrolls, a 3D couple flies through a pink sky while their photos float past, ending in a letter.

## Language

### People

**Creator**:
The person building the site as a gift, who supplies every photo, caption, date and the Letter.
_Avoid_: User, developer, owner

**Recipient**:
The Creator's girlfriend, the only person the site is made for and the only one expected to answer the Unlock question.
_Avoid_: User, viewer, visitor

### The story

**Day 1**:
The day they became a couple, and the first day the Day counter counts. It splits their story in two: How we met comes before it, and Year 1 to Year 3 come after it.
_Avoid_: Start date, day zero, the day we met

**Anniversary**:
The date exactly three years after Day 1, and the day the Recipient receives the site. Because it spans a leap day, its day number is 1,096 or 1,097, never a fixed figure.
_Avoid_: Birthday, milestone, 1,095

**Chapter**:
A named group of Memories covering one stretch of their story. There are four: How we met, then Year 1, Year 2 and Year 3, where each Year is the matching year after Day 1.
_Avoid_: Section, act, scene

**Memory**:
One photo of the two of them together, shown in a black frame with its Caption. Each Memory belongs to exactly one Chapter.
_Avoid_: Photo, post, slide

**Caption**:
The short handwritten line under a Memory, written in the Creator's own words.
_Avoid_: Label, description, title

**Intro**:
The opening title screen the Recipient sees once the Unlock question is answered, before the first Chapter begins.
_Avoid_: Splash, landing page, cover

**Letter**:
The Creator's longer message to the Recipient, shown on a cream card at the end of the story. It is the emotional high point and the last thing she reads before the Closing line.
_Avoid_: Note, message, outro

**Closing line**:
The single sentence that ends the site: "Happy 3rd anniversary, my love."
_Avoid_: Finale, footer, sign-off

### The experience

**Flyers**:
The 3D figure of the two of them holding hands, flying through the sky. They never separate and stand for the couple from the first scroll to the Letter.
_Avoid_: Silhouettes, avatars, couple model

**Flight path**:
The route the Flyers follow across the sky, made of climbs, dives, turns and one loop, so that her scrolling carries them forward.
_Avoid_: Trajectory, animation path

**Sky**:
The pink backdrop that shifts mood as the story advances: dawn pink, bright pink day, sunset, then deep rose with stars at the Letter.
_Avoid_: Background, theme

**Day counter**:
The number on screen showing how many days since Day 1 the story has reached, written as "Day N". It starts at Day 1 and ends on the Anniversary's own day number.
_Avoid_: Timer, clock

**Unlock question**:
The question only the Recipient can answer, asked before the site opens. It is a gentle lock for a surprise, not real security.
_Avoid_: Password, login, captcha
