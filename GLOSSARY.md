# Happy 3rd Year, My Love

A scroll-driven website given to the creator's girlfriend on their 3rd anniversary. As she scrolls, a 3D couple flies through a pink sky while their photos float past, ending in a letter.

## Language

### People

**Creator**:
The person building the site as a gift, who supplies every photo, caption, date and the Letter.
_Avoid_: User, developer, owner

**Recipient**:
The Creator's girlfriend, the only person the site is made for and the only one expected to answer the Unlock question.
_Avoid_: User, viewer, visitor

### The story

**Day 1**:
The day they became a couple, and the first day the Day counter counts. It splits their story in two: How we met comes before it, and Year 1 to Year 3 come after it.
_Avoid_: Start date, day zero, the day we met

**Anniversary**:
The date exactly three years after Day 1, and the day the Recipient receives the site. Because it spans a leap day, its day number is 1,096 or 1,097, never a fixed figure.
_Avoid_: Birthday, milestone, 1,095

**Chapter**:
A named group of Memories covering one stretch of their story. There are four: How we met, then Year 1, Year 2 and Year 3, where each Year is the matching year after Day 1.
_Avoid_: Section, act, scene

**Memory**:
One photo of the two of them together, shown in a deep rose-pink frame with a thin cream mat, with its Caption, arriving by Pop-out. Each Memory belongs to exactly one Chapter.
_Avoid_: Photo, post, slide

**Caption**:
The short handwritten line under a Memory, written in the Creator's own words.
_Avoid_: Label, description, title

**Write-on**:
How handwritten text appears: each stroke of the cursive letters draws itself in order as she scrolls, and undraws if she scrolls back, with no pen visible. It is used for Captions, Chapter titles, and the Letter's greeting and the Closing line.
_Avoid_: Typing effect, fade-in, pen reveal

**Wiggle**:
The playful swing of a Memory's frame, as if hanging from the corner nearest the pointer, that settles back with a spring. It is triggered by hovering, or by a tap on a phone, and only on a Memory that is in front of the Clouds.
_Avoid_: Shake, hover effect

**Chapter opener**:
The large title and date that open a Chapter: the title writes itself on, then the date under it, and both fade away before the Chapter's first Memory arrives. (The Chapter title is the title part; its date is written with the same Write-on.)
_Avoid_: Heading, banner, title card

**Intro**:
The opening title screen the Recipient sees once the Unlock question is answered, before the first Chapter begins.
_Avoid_: Splash, landing page, cover

**Letter**:
The Creator's longer message to the Recipient, shown on a cream card at the end of the story. It is the emotional high point and the last thing she reads before the Closing line.
_Avoid_: Note, message, outro

**Closing line**:
The single sentence that ends the site: "Happy 3rd anniversary, my love."
_Avoid_: Finale, footer, sign-off

### The experience

**Flyers**:
The 3D figure of the two of them holding hands, flying through the sky. They never separate and stand for the couple until the Letter. They are shown in their own colours (her rose-pink dress, his black suit) with a soft pink glow at their edges, and leave a soft pink ribbon trail with Stardust around it behind them. They spring up on the left (with a small bounce) once the first Chapter title has finished writing, and the Flight path begins there.
_Avoid_: Silhouettes, avatars, couple model

**Stardust**:
The stars, sparkles and glitter in the sky's own colours that the Flyers leave behind them, scattered around their pink ribbon trail. It runs from hot pink right behind them through coral and orange to gold at the tail, and spreads from their own width to twice that while it shrinks and fades out about 2.4 screens back. Each piece stays where it was dropped along the Flight path and twinkles there. It bursts out when the Flyers spring up, and rewinds with them if she scrolls back. Not the stars of the Sky at the Letter.
_Avoid_: Particles, confetti, rainbow (the ribbon is just "the trail")

**Flight path**:
The route the Flyers follow across the sky, made of climbs, dives, turns, one loop, Cloud passes and a Turn-around, so that her scrolling carries them forward. It runs in depth as well as across the screen.
_Avoid_: Trajectory, animation path

**Cloud pass**:
A moment on the Flight path where the Flyers fly into a Cloud, are veiled by it, and come out clear on the other side.
_Avoid_: Fly-through, hide

**Turn-around**:
A moment on the Flight path where the Flyers make a U-turn and head back, swinging toward the camera so the Recipient sees them face on.
_Avoid_: Reverse, flip

**Sky**:
The pink world the story happens in: a backdrop that shifts mood as the story advances (dawn pink, bright pink day, sunset, then deep rose with stars at the Letter), filled with Clouds at different depths.
_Avoid_: Background, theme

**Cloud**:
A soft white puff in the Sky, at its own depth. Memories and the Flyers pass behind, in front of, and into Clouds.
_Avoid_: Layer, wisp, sprite

**Pop-out**:
How a Memory arrives: it emerges from behind a Cloud and springs forward with a small bounce into its place, stays in front of the Clouds while she reads it, and leaves behind another Cloud.
_Avoid_: Reveal, fade-in, entrance

**Day counter**:
The number on screen showing how many days since Day 1 the story has reached, written as "Day N". It starts at Day 1 at the start of Year 1 and climbs to the Anniversary's own day number at the end of Year 3, and is not shown during How we met, which comes before Day 1. Every number comes from real dates, so moving Day 1 moves them all.
_Avoid_: Timer, clock

**HUD**:
The small things that stay on screen over the story: the Day counter and the Progress cue. Not the Intro, the Chapter openers or the Letter.
_Avoid_: Overlay, toolbar

**Progress cue**:
The thin line along the top edge of the screen that fills as she scrolls, showing how far through the story she is. Deliberately quiet.
_Avoid_: Progress bar, scrollbar, loading bar

**Song**:
The one piece of music the Recipient can play while she scrolls. It starts only when she taps the Sound button (never by itself), loops, and is optional: with no song set the site is silent.
_Avoid_: Soundtrack, background music, audio track

**Sound button**:
The round button in the bottom-right corner that plays and pauses the Song. Hidden when there is no Song; if the Song's file will not load, it disappears at her first tap (nothing is fetched before that).
_Avoid_: Mute button, music toggle, player

**Open in Safari hint**:
The small dismissible note shown above everything, the gate included, when the link was opened inside another app's own browser (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and similar) instead of Safari.
_Avoid_: Warning, banner, popup

**Unlock question**:
The question only the Recipient can answer, asked before the site opens. It is a gentle lock for a surprise, not real security. The full-screen pink screen that asks it, and hides the site until it is answered, is the gate.
_Avoid_: Password, login, captcha
