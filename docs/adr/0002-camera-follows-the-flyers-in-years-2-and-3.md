# The camera follows the Flyers in Years 2 and 3

Through How we met and Year 1 the camera is fixed (with a slight drift as she scrolls) and the Flight path is fitted to the screen. From the dive after Year 1 the camera moves: behind the Flyers over the Sea in Year 2 (the Chase camera), then in front of them in Year 3 (the Face-on climb), with each switch hidden inside a Cloud pass. We chose this because skimming the Sea with pictures sliding past, and a climb that faces her, cannot be shown from a fixed side view: the Flyers would have to stay inside one screen.

## Considered options

- **Keep the fixed camera and slide the world past the Flyers.** Rejected: forward travel in depth, a vertical climb and a face-on view all need the world moved every frame anyway, and the Sea's horizon, wake and reflection want a real camera.
- **Move the camera for the whole story.** Rejected: Year 1's side-on flight and Pop-out are built, tested and liked. Only the Years after Year 1 change.

## Consequences

- The Flight path has two kinds of part: fitted to the screen up to the end of Year 1, and in world units (followed by the camera) in Years 2 and 3. The join sits inside a Cloud pass, where the Flyers are veiled, so no camera jump shows.
- In Years 2 and 3 Memories are Fly-bys, hanging beside the route. They are drawn by real depth against the Flyers, which supersedes ADR 0001's "Flyers are always in front of every Memory" for those Years. That rule still holds for Pop-out in How we met and Year 1.
- Clouds and the Sea are placed along the route, not around the screen, and the Turn-around moves from before the Letter to the end of Year 2.
